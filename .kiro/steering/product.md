# Steering: Product

Ghost Data Janitor hunts "ghost data" — duplicate, long-dormant and
malformed records hiding in customer/account CSV exports — proposes a
cleanup plan, and executes it only after a human approves each action.
Audience: a data-ops manager at a small SaaS company who owns CRM hygiene
but has no tooling budget. Halloween theme stays tasteful: dark UI,
orange/purple accents, ghost vocabulary in labels only.
